- `ReachableQueue` and `MatrixPhaseBridge` share short declaration names `State` and `Consistent`. The files `TransientBridge` and `TransientParameter` each define short names `step`, `mode`, `direction`, `energy`, `mode_sum`, `reference_mode_link`, `eigenmode_stable`, `one_step_bound`; ReachableQueue also has `step`. `ReachableQueue.iterate` and `HeteroStepRef.iterate` share a short name, and WindowMax/HeteroStepRef share `window_step` and `window_all_steps`. They are under distinct fully qualified namespaces; avoid opening both without qualification. HeteroGuard deliberately shares the HeteroStepRef namespace but its declaration names are distinct. No source file name was already present in the main checkout. See the simultaneous-import result above before concluding that fully qualified declarations coexist.
- `CoherenceSpeedLimit.trace_motion_min` overlaps the main result `CoherenceMotion.trace_step_abs_le_min` (same reported-coherence min bound, under the candidate's unit-disk premise). Recommend **drop from the main bundle**, keep the reviewed inbox as independent corroboration; it does not add the main file's stronger envelopes or 1214-step results. It is nevertheless included in the full build for collision checking.
- Recommend retain **MatrixPhaseBridge** if QueueComposition is merged: it is a required accepted D6 dependency, not an already-merged main file.
- Recommend **retain WindowMax and HeteroStepRef/HeteroGuard**: for uniform b, the all-perturbation heterogeneous threshold is `c≤1/(N−1)`, while mean-zero WindowMax permits `c≤2/N`. The former covers all perturbations; the latter's sharper threshold is limited to mean-zero modes. They are complementary, not contradictory.
- Recommend archive rather than prioritize `TransientBridge` and `TransientParameter` in a focused engine-relevance merge: their Euclidean gains are largely queue-copy artifacts and must not be cited as engine amplification. They remain correct, accepted exact-real results. **Not logically superseded wholesale:** Bridge contains a c=1/4 all-time Euclidean bound; Parameter formalizes a range of root stability and one-step bounds. Keep them in the inbox, or merge only if those separate mathematical facts justify their maintenance. Parameter's exact range necessity and peak were hand arguments, not Lean theorems; its proposed all-time general-c bound remains open.
- The original gate witness and corrected gate mutation can remain audit/test evidence rather than main Lean modules. Any test integration and the owner’s choice to retain an alternative proof are separate decisions; none of these recommendations promote or delete claims.

All statements remain subject to the accepted candidates' scopes. In particular, the fixed-B synchronized model and exact-real guard assumption do not establish the Python floating-point, modulo, adaptive-weight, off-synchronous, or full-Jacobian claims. No new theorem was added by this manifest.
