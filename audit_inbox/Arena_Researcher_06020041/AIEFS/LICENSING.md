# Licensing for AIEF

This page explains, in plain words, what people may do with the material in this repository.

Copyright 2026 Jacob Brainard and AIEF contributors.

## 1. Code and Lean proofs: Apache License 2.0

All software in this repository is licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.
That covers Python scripts, shell scripts, configuration files and the Lean proofs under `lean/`.
The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

In practice, anyone may use, copy, change and share the code, including for commercial
work, as long as they:

- keep the copyright notice and a copy of the licence;
- say if they changed a file.

The licence also includes a patent grant from contributors, and it comes with **no warranty**.

## 2. Documents, write-ups and figures: CC BY 4.0

Our own written material is licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**
licence (CC BY 4.0). That covers papers and drafts we wrote, reports, notes, results write-ups,
figures and plots. The full legal text is in [`LICENSE-docs`](LICENSE-docs).

In practice, anyone may share and adapt this material for any purpose, as long as they
**give credit** (for example "AIEF, Jacob Brainard and contributors"), link to the licence, and say
if they changed anything.

## 3. Other people's work is NOT covered

Some files contain or quote papers and other material written by **other people**.
Those authors keep their copyright. Neither licence above applies to their work, and we do not
grant anyone rights in it. If you want to reuse a third-party paper, get it from its original
publisher and follow that publisher's terms.

## 4. Excerpts inside AI conversation transcripts

The `Project Histories/` and `Project Origins/` folders hold records of the project lead's
conversations with AI assistants. Some of these conversations contain excerpts from published
papers, or whole papers, that were pasted in for discussion. They are included **only to give
context for those conversations**, so readers can follow the research reasoning. They remain the
property of their authors and are not licensed by us (see section 3).

If you are an author and would like material removed, please open an issue on the repository.

## 5. Which licence applies to a file?

| What it is | Licence |
|---|---|
| Code, scripts, configs, Lean proofs, data files produced by our code | Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE`) |
| Our own documents, write-ups, figures, conversation transcripts (our own words) | CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-docs`) |
| Third-party papers, or third-party text quoted inside transcripts | Original authors' copyright; not licensed by us |

If a file carries its own licence notice, that notice takes precedence for that file.
