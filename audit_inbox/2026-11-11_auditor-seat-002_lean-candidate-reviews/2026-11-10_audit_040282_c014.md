## Blunt assessment

1. The trajectory theorems are thin wrappers. Each one is a P4a one-step fact (stepM_row_le_cap,
   gateM_nonneg, stepM_symm, stepM_diag) applied at step n. "B safety does not need frozen R" is true
   but immediate: those one-step facts already hold for every drive D. Useful packaging, not a new result.
2. The two-cycle counterexample uses pWide with γ·dt = 1, a full reset that ignores the old B,
   plus a hand-built discontinuous flip drive. Engine γ·dt is 1.5e-4. It only refutes
   "safety invariants alone imply freezing", which no AIEFS doc claims. Never cite it as evidence about
   AIEFS freezing or non-freezing.
3. The sine and phase-increment bounds are coarse (|sin| ≤ 1) and convention-free; fine as stated.
4. The cap_n vs cap_(n+1) clock point and time-0 initial condition are correct and worth keeping.
5. Witness params pWide/pTight/pQuarter have gapDelta = 0 and ε = 1; they satisfy EngineGate but
   are non-engine. Label them toy witnesses in any port.
