## Sweep Page line 227 ("0.4543 … 3/7 as the B*=0 point")

- **Fixed and correct.** In the ungated weight map, B_{n+1} = B_n + dt·((η+μ)R − μ − γB_n) (freeze_check.py:45). Its fixed point for constant R is B* = ((η+μ)R − μ)/γ (freeze_check.py:51).
  - B* = 0 exactly when R = μ/(η+μ) = 0.03/0.07 = **3/7 ≈ 0.4286**. Below that the weight decays to 0.
  - B* > B_c exactly when R > (μ+γB_c)/(η+μ) = 0.0318/0.07 ≈ **0.4543**. That is the strong-edge (persistence) threshold, consistent with ERRATA E12 and REPORT.md:213.
- One small nuance the line does not state: it is the *ungated* threshold. In the full model the min-endpoint gate can also switch an edge off; this is the "gated-OFF" certificate in RES-FREEZE-01. The line says freezing follows from this threshold "together with the decay γ and the cap", which is acceptable shorthand.
